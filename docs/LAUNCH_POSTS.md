# Tradebench launch posts (drafts, not posted)

Post these only once trade-bench.com is live **and** has tradies on it. A customer who posts a job and gets
no quotes won't come back, and tells people.

Read each subreddit's rules before posting. Most city subreddits remove or ban business self-promotion
outside a weekly or pinned promo thread; trade subreddits are stricter still. Post as yourself, say it's
your site, and answer comments.

---

## 1. Tradies first (Facebook trade groups, r/AusTradies, r/perth's promo thread if it has one)

**Title:** Perth tradies: started a job site with no lead fees, would love honest feedback

> G'day. I've started Tradebench (trade-bench.com), a Perth and WA site where homeowners, property
> managers, hotels and mine sites post repair and maintenance jobs and checked tradies quote on them.
>
> How it's different from the lead sites:
> - Free to join and free to quote. No paying for leads you don't win.
> - The customer pays into the site before you start, so the money's there.
> - You get paid when the job's signed off. We take 4% (min $15) of jobs you actually win.
>
> We check ABN, licence and insurance before anyone can quote. I'm after the first 20 or so tradies:
> tilers, plumbers, sparkies, handymen, painters and air con, especially north and east metro.
> Happy to answer anything, and keen to hear what would make you actually use it.

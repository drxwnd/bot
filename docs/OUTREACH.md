# Outreach scripts

Use these as a starting point and say them in your own words. Print flyers from `/admin/flyer/tradies` and `/admin/flyer/customers`.

## Tradies (your dad's contacts first)

**In person or on the phone**

> "G'day, it's [name], [dad]'s son. I've started a site called Tradebench for jobs around Perth: homes, hotels and some mine site work. The difference is you don't pay for leads. Customers post a job, you quote if you want it, and the customer pays up front before you start. We hold the money and release it to you when they sign off. We take 4% of the job and that's it. Can I sign you up now? It takes about 10 minutes and I just need your ABN, licence number and insurance details."

**Text to follow up**

> "Hi [name], [your name] from Tradebench. Here's the link to sign up: tradebench.au/for-tradies. Free to join, customer pays before you start, 4% only when you're paid. Ring me if you've got questions."

**If they say...**
- **"I've got enough work."** "No worries. It's handy for the quiet weeks, and jobs only come to you if they match your trade and area. There's nothing to pay unless you win one."
- **"4% is too much."** "On a $1,500 job that's $60. With lead sites you pay for every lead, whether you win the job or not. And you never chase an invoice, because the money's there before you start."
- **"I don't like paying platforms."** "Fair enough. The main thing you get is guaranteed payment. If a customer won't pay, that's your problem now. Here, they've paid before you turn up."

## Property managers

Ring agencies near where your tradies are. Ask for whoever handles maintenance.

> "Hi, my name's [name], from Tradebench, a Perth trades platform. Quick one: who looks after maintenance jobs for your rentals? ... We help property managers get quotes from checked local tradies fast. You post the job once and our tradies quote. You only pay when the job's done, and every job and invoice is in one place for each property. Could I set you up to try it with your next maintenance job? If nobody quotes within 48 hours, I'll personally find someone."

**Email to follow up**

> Subject: Quotes for your next maintenance job
>
> Hi [name],
>
> Thanks for the chat. Here's the link to post your next job: tradebench.au/start
>
> - Free to post. Quotes from local tradies whose ABN, licence and insurance we've checked.
> - Your payment is held until you sign the job off.
> - Logins for your team, and every job, quote and invoice kept per property.
>
> If nobody's quoted within 48 hours, reply to this email and I'll sort it out myself.
>
> [Your name]
> Tradebench | [phone]

**If they say...**
- **"We have our own tradies."** "Most agencies do. We're handy for when they're booked out, or for a trade you don't have someone for. There's no cost to post."
- **"Our landlords have to approve quotes."** "That works. Post the job, wait for the quotes, send the one you like to the landlord, and accept it when they say yes."

## Hotels and short-stay

> "Hi, it's [name] from Tradebench. We find checked local tradies for hotel maintenance: air con, bathrooms, painting between bookings. The job form has a section for access times and rooms you can block out, so tradies price it around your guests. Could I send you the link for your next job?"

## Mining and camps

Ring site services or camp maintenance coordinators, not the head office.

> "Hi, it's [name] from Tradebench. We're a WA platform for the smaller site jobs that don't need a tender: camp room repairs, air con, welding and plumbing in ablution blocks. You list the inductions, tickets and travel up front, and only tradies with those tickets quote. Can I send you the link?"

## Facebook community groups

Only post where the group allows business posts, and keep it plain.

> "Hi all. We've just started Tradebench, a Perth site for getting quotes from checked local tradies (ABN, licence and insurance checked). It's free to post a job, and you pay through the site, but the tradie only gets the money once you're happy it's done. We've got tilers, plumbers and sparkies covering [suburbs]. tradebench.au"

## What to track each week

You'll find these on the `/admin` page:
- **Quotes per job.** Aim for 3 or more.
- **Jobs with no quotes.** Aim for 0. Ring a tradie whenever one shows up.
- **Jobs paid.**
- **Live tradies with payouts on.**
